# kinflow-starter

`kinflow-starter` is reserved as the first candidate Spine pack. It is under
design and is not installable, released, or a definition of `kinflow-starter.v1`.

Candidate archetype content currently being developed:

- `medical_appointment`
- `lesson`
- `passport_renewal`
- `game_or_competition`
- `flight`
- `birthday`

No archetype or notification-profile definitions are committed yet. Their
semantics must not be inferred from these names. Definitions, compatibility
claims, and manifests will be added only after review of
`specs/pack-format.md`.
